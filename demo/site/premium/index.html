Why the collar works

A retriever with a spiked collar is still a retriever. It stays gentle with everyone who comes in peace. The collar does not make it dangerous, it makes it too expensive to attack: the wolf bites once and gets hurt.

Spikes402 does the same for a website. Readers and well-behaved clients pass without noticing. A scraper that wants to take everything for free has to bring something real: a few sats, or some electricity. Cheap for one page, ruinous for millions.

You got here by paying or by working, once, for this one page. That is the whole idea.
